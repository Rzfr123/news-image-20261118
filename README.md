Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118

## Stories

- **Three leaders, one summit** — Trump lands in Shenzhen as Xi and Putin meet a day ahead of the first US–China–Russia three-way talks.
- **Iran hits Kuwait, Bahrain** — Tehran strikes a US base and Gulf sites after US strikes on Qeshm Island.
- **Kerch Bridge hit again** — Ukraine strikes the Crimea link; part of the road span collapses.
- **Democrats take Senate** — Peltola unseats Sullivan in Alaska; Democrats lead 56–44.
- **Truce at risk** — The Board of Peace warns the Gaza ceasefire is at risk of collapse.
- Briefs: Ebola passes 11,000 cases in DR Congo; Anthropic raises a record $81.3bn; the US loses measles-free status after 26 years.

## How it was made

`render.py` draws the front page programmatically with Pillow — no AI image generation, so every headline is exact. `main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` and verifies its SHA-256 digest.
